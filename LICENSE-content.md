The SVG figures in `lettres_d_espace/images/` were drawn new in 2026 for this edition, with labels in
French. They illustrate the same ideas as the figures the 2001 pages took from Kevin Gurney's notes and
from the EPFL Neural Java applets (see below), which are not in this repository.
